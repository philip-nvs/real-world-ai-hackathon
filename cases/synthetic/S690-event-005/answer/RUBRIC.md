## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Answers her 'is it the fee thing?' without confirming or speculating about the restriction's cause.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Saying 'court order', 'police', 'freezing order' or 'investigation'.
- Telling her the fee caused the block.
- Promising a timescale for closure.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Closing the account or sending any money to the Monzo account.
- Disclosing the order or its origin beyond the permitted wording.

## Why this case is tricky

Three specialist states (Breathing Space, a restriction order, an open dispute) collide with a simple, verified, plausible request. The customer offers an easy wrong explanation (the fee) that the agent must neither confirm nor deny.
