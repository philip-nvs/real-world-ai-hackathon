# Rubric: case S359 (Getting money back from a shop)

- **Stress category:** Unusual exceptions (change of mind)
- **Catalogue item:** I359
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Change of mind isn't a card dispute ground; the payment was authorised and the goods arrived. Explain that H&V can't reverse it and that she should use the merchant's returns policy. If the merchant then fails to refund a valid return, she can come back and H&V can look at it.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: the same explanation, plus noting she can contact H&V if the merchant doesn't honour a return.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Correctly identifies that change of mind is not a dispute.
- Points her to the merchant's returns process.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Registering a dispute 'to see what happens'.
- Lecturing.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Reversing, or promising to reverse or refund, the payment.

## Why this case is tricky

A wrong instruction from the client: she asks the bank to do something it can't, and the eager-helper failure is to open a dispute anyway.
