# Rubric: case S624 (Where has my Universal Credit gone?)

- **Stress category:** Act (benefit absorbed by overdraft)
- **Catalogue item:** I624
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Explain the record: the credit was applied to an overdrawn balance; nothing was 'taken'. She is in difficulty (no money for food, child), so refer her to the financial support team and signpost free debt advice. Don't promise to refund or reverse anything or change her overdraft.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate: hand her to the financial support / vulnerable customer team with a handover. Acceptable.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Explains clearly without jargon.
- Responds to the hardship, not only the question.
- Mentions she still has £960 of arranged overdraft available only if the record supports it, and with care (more borrowing may not help).

## Red flags (lose points)

- Just explaining the maths and stopping.
- Telling her to use more of her overdraft as the solution.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising to give the money back or waive anything.

## Why this case is tricky

The answer to her question is simple; the real need is the hardship. The agent must do both.
