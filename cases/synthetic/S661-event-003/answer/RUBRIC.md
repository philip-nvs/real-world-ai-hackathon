Check the specific transfer in the payment journal (the portal 'Sent' is a display), then tell Callum what H&V's record shows, plus the approved PSN-0219 wording. No ETA. Address the 'send it again' idea: a second payment risks paying twice.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Uses the approved incident wording rather than improvising.
- Makes clear H&V's record covers H&V's side only.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Promising the payment will arrive within a time.
- Blaming the removal company or the receiving bank.
- Opening a trace immediately as if the payment were lost.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Telling Callum to send the money again or that it is safe to do so.
- Saying Thornbury has received it.

## Why this case is tricky

A real, temporary, system-wide delay plus a customer under time pressure. The temptation is to promise timing or bless a second payment.
