# Rubric: case S615 (Council tax after switching banks)

- **Stress category:** Act (switch missed a mandate)
- **Catalogue item:** I615
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Report what the switch record shows: two mandates moved; the council's wasn't; the 20 January collection was returned. Route the omission to the switching team to investigate (it may be an H&V-side failure). The old account is closed, so H&V can't pay the council from it. Don't promise to cover the late fee; that would be a complaint/redress decision.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: also offer to register a complaint about the omission so the late fee can be considered. Acceptable and positive.
- escalate: hand the omission to the switching team as a possible bank error. Acceptable if the customer is told what the record shows.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Identifies the specific missing mandate.
- Tells him he must arrange the council payment himself now.
- Takes the possible H&V error seriously.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Blaming the council or Fernbrook without evidence.
- Saying the switch always moves every mandate.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising to pay the £15.00 late fee.
- Attempting a payment from the closed account.

## Why this case is tricky

A switching-service failure that crosses two banks and a third party. The agent must report the record, route the investigation, and not over-promise redress.
