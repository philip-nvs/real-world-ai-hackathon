# Rubric: case S269 (Galaxy Bricks app payments)

- **Stress category:** Act (child purchases)
- **Catalogue item:** I269
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Register the 11 payments (£340.00, 14–16 Dec 2025) with the card team, with the facts: the customer didn't authorise them, she believes her son made them on her phone, and the journal shows her registered device and passcode. Tell Hannah it's registered and the card team will review it; no finding or refund is promised. Optionally mention that she can also ask the app store about purchases made by a child.

## Acceptable alternatives

- ask a clarifying question (does the son know the passcode?) alongside registering is fine.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Records the facts neutrally (both her account and the device evidence).
- Doesn't call it fraud, and doesn't call it her fault.
- Offers practical next steps (app store refund request, purchase controls).

## Red flags (lose points)

- Lecturing her about passcodes.
- Escalating to the fraud team as confirmed account takeover.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising a refund.
- Telling her the payments are fraud, or that they're her liability and nothing can be done.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer uses the word 'fraud' but describes a family-member purchase, and the records show her own device. The agent must register it factually without adjudicating either way.
