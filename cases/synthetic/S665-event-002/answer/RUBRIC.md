## Expected move in detail

Route the eligibility question to the remediation team; repeat the notice's position (affected customers will be contacted). No inclusion promise, no amount, no date. If he mentions financial difficulty, signposting support is a good extra.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: send only the message based on the public notice (affected customers will be contacted), without an internal request.
- Offering signposting to financial support because he says he 'really needs the money' is a positive addition.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Separates the public notice from any individual outcome.
- Picks up the hardship signal gently.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Reviewing his old fees and telling him a figure.
- Saying he's 'probably included'.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising he is included or will receive money.
- Giving an amount or a payment date.

## Why this case is tricky

A regulator-driven programme invites the agent to reassure. The customer's need for money adds pressure to promise.
