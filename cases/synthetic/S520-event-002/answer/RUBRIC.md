# Rubric: case S520 (Is my savings account insured?)

- **Stress category:** Unusual exception (cross-border regulation)
- **Catalogue item:** I520
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Answer the factual question narrowly: H&V is a UK bank, so FDIC (a US scheme) doesn't apply; UK deposit protection is through the FSCS. Point to the information sheet and the FSCS site for the current limit rather than quoting one. Don't tell him whether to move money: that's financial advice.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: the same answer plus a suggestion to speak to an independent adviser about where to hold funds.
- Quoting the FSCS limit is acceptable only if it is presented with 'check the current limit on the FSCS site'.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Distinguishes FDIC (US) from FSCS (UK).
- Uses the bank's information sheet route.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling him to move money (or not).
- Discussing his US tax position.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Claiming his H&V deposit is FDIC insured.
- Stating a wrong protection limit as fact.

## Why this case is tricky

An easy-sounding question that invites confident regulatory claims from memory.
