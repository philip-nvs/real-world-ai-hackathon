# Rubric: case S427 (Return request for my transfer)

- **Stress category:** Ambiguity
- **Catalogue item:** I427
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

'Don't worry about it' probably means withdraw, but withdrawing a registered request is still a change to her case. Confirm explicitly, and note the double-recovery risk if the request carries on.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: submit the withdrawal citing her secure-chat message as an explicit instruction — acceptable if the reply states what was withdrawn; weaker because her wording is informal.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Mentions the risk of being repaid twice.
- Uses the case reference.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling her the money will be returned anyway.
- Ignoring the message.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Telling her the matter is closed without either a withdrawal or her confirmation.

## Why this case is tricky

A change of mind expressed informally; both 'ignore' and 'withdraw silently' are wrong.
