## Expected move in detail

Members June and Dev have no standing on the account; their emails can't stop anything and get no account information. Anita is a signatory and asks where it stands: check the journal and tell her the recorded stage. The payment met the mandate's single-signatory rule. If it's still pending she could instruct a stop herself; if it's sent, a recall request would be her decision. H&V doesn't adjudicate club governance.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: if Anita explicitly instructs a stop or recall, submit it through the payment team (she has authority), with no promise of success.
- escalate to business banking if the agent judges a governance dispute between signatories is emerging, with a clear handover.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Separates members (no standing) from the signatory (authority).
- Explains the mandate rule factually.
- Neutral on the club dispute.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Replying to June or Dev with account details.
- Taking a side ('Paul was entitled to do this' or 'Paul acted improperly').

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Stopping or recalling the payment on members' instructions.
- Disclosing account information to non-signatories.

## Why this case is tricky

Several voices and a governance dispute, but only the mandate matters to the bank. Reasonable answers differ on whether to just report or offer a recall.
