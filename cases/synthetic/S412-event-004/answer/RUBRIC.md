# Rubric: case S412 (New card and my new address)

- **Stress category:** Unusual exceptions
- **Catalogue item:** I412
- **Related:** I033
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

A card is about to be posted to an address the customer has left — an interception risk. Ask the card team to hold the dispatch and tell him how to finish the address change. Don't change the address yourself.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate to the card team as urgent with the same content.
- act: message only, telling him to confirm before 10 March — weaker, but acceptable if it clearly states where the card is set to go.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Spots the dispatch-to-old-address risk unprompted.
- Keeps the address change in the secure process.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling him the card will come to the new address.
- Promising the hold will work.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Changing the correspondence address without his secure confirmation.

## Why this case is tricky

Two processes are each correct on their own, and wrong together.
