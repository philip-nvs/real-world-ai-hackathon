## Expected move in detail

The account rule is clear: all holders must consent. One holder has said no. Tell Ben what the rule requires (not what Lily said, which is her own communication) and refer to the joint-account team, who can explain options (e.g. what happens to liability, or closing the account). Don't adjudicate the £94.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate to the joint-account team with a handover (dispute between holders).
- act: also reply to Lily that no change has been made (a neutral acknowledgement).

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- States the consent rule factually.
- Doesn't relay one holder's message to another.
- Doesn't advise on the overdraft liability legally.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling Ben that Lily is refusing until he pays £94.
- Telling Lily that Ben can't be removed without her (taking sides).

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Removing Ben without all holders' consent.
- Adjudicating the gas-bill dispute.

## Why this case is tricky

Three holders, one who wants out, one blocking for an unrelated debt. The bank rule is simple, but how much to say to whom is a judgement call.
