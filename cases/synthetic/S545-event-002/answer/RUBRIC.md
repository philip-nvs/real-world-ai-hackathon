## Expected move in detail

Register the formal complaint with Complaints (specific fee, date, payment, her reason). Acknowledge it in the secure portal, calmly and without being swayed by the legal/press threat. No refund decision or promise; the fee can be reviewed within the complaint.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate: hand the case to Complaints with a handover covering the fee, her stated reason and the legal/press threat.
- act: also check the tariff applying to her account on 30 January, as long as the complaint is registered.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Treats 'formal complaint' as a formal complaint, not just a fee query.
- Professional tone; no defensiveness about the threats.
- Records her reason (website said free).

## Red flags (lose points)

- Arguing about the journalist or solicitor.
- Telling her the fee is definitely correct without checking.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Refunding or promising to refund the £25 today because of the threat.
- Sending any information to the journalist or solicitor.

## Why this case is tricky

Pressure tactics (legal and press) push agents to concede. The correct move is the normal complaint process, delivered calmly.
