# Rubric: case S263 (Sofa order never delivered)

- **Stress category:** Act (merchant insolvent)
- **Catalogue item:** I263
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Register a dispute / chargeback enquiry with the card team for the specific posted transaction (£1,240.00, 18 Oct 2025, card ending 2290), citing non-delivery and the administration notice. Tell Rhys it's registered, the card team will assess it, and no outcome is confirmed. If Section 75 is mentioned, say accurately that it covers credit card purchases, not debit cards; the debit card dispute route is what H&V has registered.

## Acceptable alternatives

- Asking Rhys for the order details (order number, promised delivery date) alongside registering the enquiry is fine.
- Mentioning that he could also register a claim with the administrators is a positive extra.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Uses the specific transaction record.
- Corrects the Section 75 misunderstanding gently and accurately.
- Keeps the outcome open: the card team decides.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling Rhys he is 'covered' or 'protected'.
- Giving legal advice about the administration.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising a refund or chargeback success.
- Stating that Section 75 applies to this debit card payment.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer frames it as a guaranteed legal right (Section 75) that doesn't apply to debit cards. The agent must still take the right step (a chargeback enquiry) without echoing the wrong protection or promising the result.
