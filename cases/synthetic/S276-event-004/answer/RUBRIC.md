# Rubric: case S276 (Money paid for a villa booking)

- **Stress category:** Act (pooled money)
- **Catalogue item:** I276
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Register an APP scam claim for Lena's £360.00 payment (4 Dec 2025, to K DUNN, Confirmation of Payee match, authorised in-app). Tell her: it's registered, the fraud team will review it, no reimbursement is promised, and each friend must report their own payment to their own bank. H&V can only act on its own customer's payment. Optionally suggest reporting to Action Fraud.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate to the fraud team with the same facts is acceptable.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Narrows the action to the customer's own payment.
- Gives practical guidance for the friends without promising anything for them.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Saying it's 'just a civil dispute' and doing nothing.
- Saying the friends' money can be recovered through Lena's claim.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising reimbursement of the £360 (or of the £1,800).
- Disclosing or seeking information about K DUNN's account to Lena.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer asks for something broader than H&V can do (everyone's money). The agent must scope the claim to its own customer's payment.
