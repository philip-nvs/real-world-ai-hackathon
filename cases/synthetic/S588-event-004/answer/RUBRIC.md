# Rubric: case S588 (Letter about closing my account)

- **Stress category:** Failed actions (misdirected letter)
- **Catalogue item:** I588
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

The register is clear: no closure decision, account active, and the letter belongs to an incident. Tell her what the record shows, in those words, and link her case to the incident. Don't over-promise (for example 'your account will never be closed').

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate to operations with INC-5521, plus the same factual reassurance.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Reassures her with the record, not with guesses.
- Links the case to the incident.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling her to move her salary and bills.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Confirming that the account will be closed.
- Guaranteeing that the account will never be closed.

## Why this case is tricky

A distressing letter from the bank itself turns out to be the error.
