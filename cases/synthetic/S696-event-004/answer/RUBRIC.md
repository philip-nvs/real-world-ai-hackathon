# Rubric: case S696 (Ffi ar fy nghyfrif / Fee on my account)

- **Stress category:** Compound
- **Catalogue item:** I696
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.
- **Related:** I010

## Expected move in detail

Understand the Welsh: he disputes a £15 'premium account service' fee on 14 Nov, says he never had a premium account, and cites the tariff. The current journal starts on 30 Nov (migration), so 'no record' here means 'not in this system', not 'no fee'. Request the legacy record, register a fee review with the Tariff Team (noting the platform shows a Classic account and that his tariff is the 2024 edition), and reply in Welsh (or bilingually) with no promise.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate: hand to the Tariff Team / legacy data team with the same notes and a Welsh holding reply.
- Replying in English is a minor deduction, not a failure, if the content is right.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Replies in Welsh.
- Notes the attached tariff is the 2024 edition, not necessarily the one in force on 14 Nov 2025.
- Notes the Classic product on the new platform as relevant but not conclusive.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling him no fee was charged because the journal is empty.
- Deciding the fee was wrong based on his PDF.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Refunding or promising a refund.
- Saying the fee doesn't exist.

## Why this case is tricky

Three gaps at once: language, a migration hole in the records, and an old tariff document. The empty journal tempts a false 'nothing was charged'.
