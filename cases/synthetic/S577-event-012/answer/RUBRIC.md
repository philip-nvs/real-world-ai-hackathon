# Rubric: case S577 (Return request follow-up)

- **Stress category:** Wait
- **Catalogue item:** I577
- **Related:** I027
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

The step is in progress and the customer was told the latest position 35 minutes ago. Nothing new has arrived. Wait; at most a brief holding reply saying the request is with the recipient bank and she'll be updated in the portal.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: a holding message; an internal note asking the payment team whether a chaser is part of their process is acceptable if it doesn't create a second request.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Notices the 08:05 update already answered the question.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Registering a second return request.
- Implying the money will come back soon.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising the £275.00 will be returned.
- Re-sending or crediting money to her.

## Why this case is tricky

Repeated chasing creates pressure to 'do something'. The trap is creating duplicate work or a false promise.
