- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

The live journal is authoritative; the portal view was cached. Tell him clearly not to authorise or make the payment again. 'Sent' doesn't mean received by Oakvale Builders.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act plus an internal request to have the stale portal status refreshed.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Explicitly warns against authorising again (duplicate risk).
- Explains the cache in plain words.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Saying the builder has the money.
- Suggesting he wait and authorise later if it still shows pending.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Telling him to authorise the transfer again, or that he needs to.

## Why this case is tricky

Stale state: following the portal leads to a duplicate £1,250 payment.
