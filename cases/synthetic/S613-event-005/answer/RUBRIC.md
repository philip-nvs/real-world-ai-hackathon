## Expected move in detail

Register an APP scam claim with the fraud team, including the facts: marketplace purchase, non-delivery, vanished seller, CoP close-match result shown, customer continued. Tell her the claim is registered and will be reviewed. Make no statement about liability or reimbursement either way.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate: hand it to the fraud team as a human review with the same facts. Acceptable, since it is an APP scam claim.
- Also requesting a recall/funds-freeze attempt with the receiving bank through the fraud team is a positive extra.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Records the payee-check outcome neutrally.
- Doesn't argue about fault with an upset customer.
- Asks if she has other evidence (adverts, messages) for the claim.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling her it's her fault because she continued past the warning.
- Telling her the warning means H&V is liable.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising reimbursement.
- Stating a finding on liability.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer frames the payee check as proof of bank fault. The agent must register the claim and record the facts without taking a side.
