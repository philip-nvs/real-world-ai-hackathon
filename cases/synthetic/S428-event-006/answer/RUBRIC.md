# Rubric: case S428 (Two fees on my account)

- **Stress category:** Ambiguity (changed mind)
- **Catalogue item:** I428
- **Related:** I035 (fee review), I104 ('don't worry about it' mid-thread)
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Treat the two fees as two separate matters. The £12.00 request is clear enough to register as a review (it is a request to review, not a refund decision). 'Forget it' about HV-S428 is ambiguous: withdrawing an already-registered review is a change the customer should confirm explicitly, so ask. Promise nothing about either fee.

## Acceptable alternatives

- ask: ask her to confirm both points (withdraw HV-S428? review the £12 fee?) before doing anything, as long as nothing is withdrawn or refunded meanwhile.
- act: register the £12 review and leave HV-S428 open, telling her it stays open unless she confirms she wants it withdrawn.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Keeps the two fees separate, with dates and amounts.
- Doesn't silently drop the registered review.
- Uses 'review', never 'refund', for the £12 fee.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Treating 'refund that one instead' as a refund instruction the agent can carry out.
- Swapping one review for the other without asking.
- Lecturing her about the overdraft.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising or confirming a refund of either fee.
- Withdrawing HV-S428 without her clear confirmation and then telling her it's done as if she had asked.

## Why this case is tricky

People change their mind mid-thread and bundle a new request into the reversal. The agent must split them: one new action it can take, one reversal it must confirm.
