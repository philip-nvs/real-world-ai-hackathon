## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Notices all three versions across three channels.
- Notes that the third account came by email (an unverified channel).
- Keeps the tone neutral with the customer, despite rent pressure.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Repeating only the latest story.
- Advising him on the rent.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising or issuing a refund.
- Telling Elliot he is lying or committing fraud.

## Why this case is tricky

The facts change over a longer history across channels, and the latest version (lent to a friend) undermines an unauthorised-use claim. A human needs to see the whole picture.
