MILLBROOK ATHLETIC CLUB — ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
Date: 15 January 2026, Millbrook Community Centre
Members present: 38

Item 6 — Club name
Motion: that the club be renamed "Millbrook Harriers Running Club" with effect from 1 February 2026, and the constitution amended accordingly.
Proposed: N. Barraclough. Seconded: J. Whitlam. Carried (35 for, 1 against, 2 abstentions).

Item 7 — Subscriptions
Annual adult subscription remains £45, payable by bank transfer by 28 February.

Item 8 — Any other business
Reminder to bring hi-vis for the winter Tuesday runs. Cake rota to be circulated.

Signed: N. Barraclough, Secretary
