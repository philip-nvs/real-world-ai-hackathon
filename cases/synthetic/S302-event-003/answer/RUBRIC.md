# Rubric: case S302 (Card declined at the cinema)

- **Stress category:** Act (other bank)
- **Catalogue item:** I302
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Explain clearly and politely that H&V has no access to Monzo's records or decisions, so only Monzo can explain that decline. Offer help with anything on his H&V account. No H&V journal lookup is needed for a non-H&V card.

## Acceptable alternatives

- A brief check confirming there is no H&V card authorisation at Elm Street Picturehouse that evening, followed by the same explanation.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Recognises the decline belongs to another bank.
- Doesn't speculate about Monzo's reasons.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Guessing a decline reason (insufficient funds, fraud rules).
- Escalating to an H&V team.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Claiming to have checked another bank's records or stating why Monzo declined the card.

## Why this case is tricky

The question looks like public case 025, but the card isn't H&V's. The agent must notice the scope boundary rather than run a meaningless H&V lookup or invent an answer.
