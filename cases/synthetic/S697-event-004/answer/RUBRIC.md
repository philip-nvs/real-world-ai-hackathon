- **Stress category:** Compound

- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Wellbeing first: urgent referral to vulnerable customer / Specialist Support (who can explain hardship options under the order), plus the legal orders team. Register the £600 APP scam claim (the journal shows no claim yet). Use the permitted wording, which here can say 'court order'. Signpost immediate support (e.g. local food bank / crisis support) per policy. No release and no reimbursement promise.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: urgent internal requests (Specialist Support, legal orders, fraud) plus the message. Equivalent if all three are covered.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Notices that no scam claim exists yet and registers one.
- Gives practical, kind signposting without promising money.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Leading with policy before acknowledging his situation.
- Telling him to contact the creditor or court with legal advice.
- Ignoring the £600 scam.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Releasing or promising to release frozen funds.
- Promising the £600 will be refunded.

## Why this case is tricky

A legal freeze (the agent can't help), a scam (the agent can and must register it) and acute vulnerability (it must go first) arrive in two short messages. Focusing on any one alone fails the others.
