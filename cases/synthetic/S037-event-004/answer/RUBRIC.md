- **Stress category:** Conflicting information
- **Catalogue item:** I011
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Check the card journal for a replacement order for Leah's card before saying anything. The previous note says 'done' but there's no record of an order. Then report what the journal actually shows (and, if no order exists, correct the earlier message and raise the replacement properly).

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: internal request to the card team to confirm whether a replacement was ordered.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Doesn't trust the earlier internal note as fact.
- Is ready to admit the earlier message may have been wrong.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Repeating 'it's on its way'.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising a delivery date.
- Ordering a second card without checking (risk of two live cards).

## Why this case is tricky

An internal note is a claim too. Agents tend to trust their own side's notes.
