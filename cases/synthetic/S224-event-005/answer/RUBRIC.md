# Rubric: case S224 (Subscription on my new card)

- **Stress category:** Act (continuous payment authority)
- **Catalogue item:** I224
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

She can cancel a continuous payment authority with her bank, and she gave that instruction explicitly on a verified call (event-003). Submit the CPA cancellation for LUMENSTREAM LTD on card ending 5521, citing event-003. Tell her via the secure portal that it's submitted, that it stops H&V paying future recurring card payments to Lumenstream (per the record), and that her agreement with Lumenstream and the past charges are separate.

## Acceptable alternatives

- Explaining also how the card updater moved the payment to the new card is a plus.
- Offering to register a card-team enquiry for the 14 February £11.99 is fine if no refund is promised.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Knows the customer can cancel a CPA through the bank; doesn't send her back to the merchant only.
- Cites the verified instruction event.
- Explains why the payment followed her to the new card.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling her only the merchant can stop it.
- Blocking or replacing the card again as the fix.
- Saying her Lumenstream contract is cancelled.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising a refund of the subscription charges.

## Why this case is tricky

Many agents wrongly say 'contact the merchant'. The bank can stop a CPA, the instruction is valid, and the right move is to act.
