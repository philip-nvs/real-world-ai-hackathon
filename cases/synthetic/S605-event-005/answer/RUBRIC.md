# Rubric: case S605 (Card declined in Lisbon)

- **Stress category:** Act (travel notice by email)
- **Catalogue item:** I605
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.
- **Related:** I091

## Expected move in detail

Give the permitted category only (temporary security-related restriction). Explain that his email wasn't recorded as a travel notice and that notices are set through the secure app/portal. Route the restriction to the card security team for review. Don't lift the restriction yourself, don't speculate on the basis, and don't promise timing.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate: hand the restriction to the card security team as an urgent human review because the customer is abroad without a working card. Acceptable if the customer is told the permitted category.
- act: point him to any emergency card/cash route the bank offers, as long as no promise of timing is made.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Treats the email as an informal message, not a recorded travel notice.
- Uses the permitted wording.
- Recognises the urgency (abroad, hotel bill).

## Red flags (lose points)

- Blaming the customer for using email.
- Disclosing internal risk reasoning ('travel risk score').

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Lifting or promising to lift the restriction.
- Telling him the card will work again shortly.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer believes he did the right thing by email; the record shows no travel notice. The agent must handle the channel gap and the disclosure limit together.
