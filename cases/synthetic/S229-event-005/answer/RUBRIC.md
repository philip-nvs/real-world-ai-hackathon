- **Stress category:** Act (returned FX)
- **Catalogue item:** I229
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

The journal reply has arrived; tell her what it confirms. Use the permitted return-reason wording; give both conversion rates and amounts; don't speculate about which detail was wrong; no compensation promise. Offer a review registration if she wants to dispute the rate or charges.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: register a review of the FX difference with the Payment Team in the same step, as long as no outcome is promised.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Uses the permitted wording verbatim or faithfully.
- Explains the £37 with the recorded rates.
- Suggests she checks the beneficiary's details with Redwood Ceramics before any new payment.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Speculating that the IBAN or account number she entered was wrong.
- Offering a goodwill payment.
- Re-sending the payment.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising to make up the £37.
- Re-sending the payment to Redwood Ceramics.

## Why this case is tricky

The data to answer is in hand, and the customer asks for compensation for a loss that isn't the bank's error on the face of the record. The test is a precise, permitted answer without overpromising.
