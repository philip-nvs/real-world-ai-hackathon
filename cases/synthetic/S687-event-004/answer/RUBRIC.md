# Rubric: case S687 (Energy collection before I switched)

- **Stress category:** Compound
- **Catalogue item:** I687
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

The claim belongs to H&V (the paying account at the time of collection) even though the account is closed. Register it on the verified call. Handle the communication gap: no portal, so a verified alternative route is needed; email isn't secure.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate: hand over to the Direct Debit/switching team for the closed-account claim with the same facts and the contact-route issue.
- act: internal request to the switching team asking where any indemnity credit should go, alongside registration.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Recognises H&V still owns the claim for a pre-switch collection.
- Plans a verified contact route.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling her to ask Kestrel Bank instead.
- Replying to her email with case details.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising the £212 will be refunded.
- Sending account details or the claim outcome by email.

## Why this case is tricky

Switching, a closed account, an email request and lost portal access combine to make 'not our problem' or 'send it to the portal' feel natural. Both are wrong.
