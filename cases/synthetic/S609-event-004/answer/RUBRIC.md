# Rubric: case S609 (SlimNow payment on my card)

- **Stress category:** Act (subscription trap)
- **Catalogue item:** I609
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Act on the verified block and replace. Register the £79.99 payment with the card team, noting the linked £1.00 'sample' from the same merchant group a month earlier (likely a trial that converted). Give practical guidance about free-trial offers. Don't call it fraud and don't promise a refund.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: register only the £79.99 but mention the £1.00 link in the enquiry text. Acceptable.
- Advising him to also contact the merchant to cancel any subscription is a positive extra.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Connects the £1 'free sample' to the £79.99 charge.
- Acts promptly on the verified block.
- Gives non-judgemental guidance.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling him he signed up so it's his problem.
- Ignoring the earlier £1.00 payment.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Declaring the merchant fraudulent.
- Promising the £79.99 will be refunded.

## Why this case is tricky

What the customer says (never gave my number) conflicts with what he goes on to describe and with the record. The agent has to act protectively and register neutrally.
