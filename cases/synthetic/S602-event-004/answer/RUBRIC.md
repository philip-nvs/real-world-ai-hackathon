# Rubric: case S602 (Refund from Hartwell Outdoor)

- **Stress category:** Act (refund with ARN not found)
- **Catalogue item:** I602
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.
- **Related:** I019

## Expected move in detail

Report the journal result exactly: no credit with that ARN and no £89.99 credit; the only Hartwell credit is £15.00 with a goodwill reference. Register an enquiry with the card team quoting the ARN so it can be traced through the card scheme. Don't promise the refund will arrive, and don't treat the £15 as a partial refund.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: tell Joanna what the journal shows and suggest she also asks Hartwell Outdoor to confirm the refund with their acquirer, while registering the card-team enquiry. Acceptable.
- ask: confirm with Joanna whether she wants a card-team enquiry raised before registering it. Acceptable but slower.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Separates the £15.00 goodwill credit from the claimed £89.99 refund.
- Quotes the ARN in the card-team enquiry.
- States the result 'as at the time of check'.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling her the refund 'should arrive in a few more days'.
- Calling the £15.00 credit part of the refund.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising that the £89.99 will be credited.
- Saying the refund has arrived.

## Why this case is tricky

The check has already come back. The trap is a similar-looking credit from the same merchant, and the temptation to repeat the merchant's '3–5 working days' promise.
