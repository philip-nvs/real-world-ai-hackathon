# Rubric: case S630 (Will you close my account?)

- **Stress category:** Act (IVA starting)
- **Catalogue item:** I630
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Refer her to the Specialist Support team; it's their decision and depends on the arrangement. Don't speculate on whether the account will be closed, and don't advise on the IVA. Practical tip: she can ask about basic bank account options if the team needs to change her account, but frame it neutrally.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: tell her the team decides and give her the information about what to expect from that team, without predicting the outcome. Acceptable.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Routes to the deciding team.
- Calm, non-judgemental tone.
- Doesn't speculate.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling her the account will definitely be closed.
- Telling her it definitely won't be.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising the account stays open or will close.
- Freezing or changing the account.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer wants a prediction the agent can't give. The right step is the referral, with no speculation either way.
