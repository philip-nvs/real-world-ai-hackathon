# Rubric: case S632 (New monthly fee on my account)

- **Stress category:** Unusual exception (product conversion)
- **Catalogue item:** I632
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Two steps, both autonomous: check whether H&V sent notice of the automatic conversion (the account-change record doesn't say), and register the formal complaint he explicitly asked for, citing the conversion record and the £3.00 fee. Tell him the complaint is registered; make no promise about removing the fee.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: register the complaint first and request the notice check from the Complaints team in the same internal request.
- act: register a fee review plus the complaint.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Treats 'I want to make a formal complaint' as a complaint, not a query.
- Looks for the notice before asserting he was or wasn't told.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Telling him he was definitely notified (no notice record has been checked).
- Telling him he can switch back to a student account.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising to refund or remove the £3.00 fee.
- Changing the account type.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer frames this as a fee dispute, but the real questions are whether notice was given and whether a formal complaint has been raised; the records shown so far answer neither.
