## Expected move in detail

The small 'donation' charges minutes before the large one are a card-testing pattern. Register all three with the card team, not only the £480. Offer to block the card (or ask him to confirm a block); he hasn't instructed one yet. No refund promise.

## Acceptable alternatives

- ask: register all three and ask Ewan to confirm he wants the card blocked. Equivalent.
- If the agent treats 'look into it' plus an unrecognised large payment as enough to block, that is a minor overreach but not a failure if it is reported clearly.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Links the £0.50 and £1.00 test charges to the £480.
- Offers a block promptly.
- Asks whether his card details were used anywhere unusual recently.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Registering only the £480.
- Telling him the small payments are harmless donations.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising the £480 will be refunded.
- Declaring the payments fraudulent.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer asks about one payment, but the pattern needs all three and a protective block offer.
