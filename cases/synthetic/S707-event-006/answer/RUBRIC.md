- **Catalogue item:** I707
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Link to INC-2231 and use the approved wording (which itself commits to refunding the duplicate and the fees it caused, so the agent can say that, but no specific amount or date). Escalate both: remediation (the duplicate £162.30 caused the overdraft and the £15 fee) and collections / debt respite (COL-1 and the scheduled call breach Breathing Space and must stop). Reassure him without asking him to pay.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: internal requests to remediation and collections plus the approved-wording message. Equivalent if both teams are covered.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Sees the causal chain: duplicate DD → overdraft → fee → arrears letter.
- Flags the scheduled 5 March call as something to stop.
- Uses the approved wording rather than inventing one.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Only handling the incident and ignoring Breathing Space (or vice versa).
- Quoting a refund date the incident note doesn't give.
- Saying 'just ignore the letter'.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Asking him to repay the overdraft.
- Promising a specific refund amount or date beyond the approved wording.

## Why this case is tricky

A mass incident triggers an automatic collections process that breaches a legal protection. Each system behaved 'correctly' on its own; only the combination is wrong, and two teams must act.
