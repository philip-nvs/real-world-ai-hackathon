# Rubric: case S614 (Payment to the wrong account number)

- **Stress category:** Act (return request during payee-check outage)
- **Catalogue item:** I614
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

She has explicitly asked, on a verified call, for a return request on a mis-keyed payment. Register it with the Payment Team, recording the account number used and the payee-check outage. Tell her the request is registered and its outcome isn't guaranteed. Don't pay the window cleaner again for her.

## Acceptable alternatives

- act: also tell her she can pay Clearview Windows herself if she chooses, making clear H&V can't guarantee the first £140 will come back. Acceptable as long as it isn't framed as 'pay again, you'll get the first one back'.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Records the payee-check outage as a fact for the payment team.
- Uses the exact account number from the journal.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Saying the outage means H&V will refund her.
- Recommending she 'just pays again'.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Promising the £140.00 will be returned.

## Why this case is tricky

A plain misdirected payment, complicated by a scheme outage the customer may later argue about. The agent should record it, not interpret it.
