# Rubric: case S341 (Credit missing from my June statement)

- **Stress category:** Conflicting information (wrong attachment)
- **Catalogue item:** I341
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

The attachment is the May 2025 statement, not June: it shows the May £120.00 credit and a May closing balance of £636.10, not the £512.40 she quotes. Don't reason from it about June. Check H&V's own incoming-payment records for June 2025 (a credit of £120.00 from P Calder around 15 June), then report what the records show.

## Acceptable alternatives

- ask: point out that the attached file is the May statement and ask her to confirm the period, as long as the agent also offers to (or does) check June in H&V's records.
- act: check the June statement record in the statements service as well as the incoming journal.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Notices that the attachment's period is May 2025, not June.
- Notices that the attachment does show a £120.00 credit (15 May), so it doesn't prove anything about June.
- Checks H&V's records rather than the customer's document.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Saying the June credit is missing because the attachment doesn't show it.
- Quoting the June closing balance from the customer's message as if it were confirmed.
- Telling her to ask her father to pay again.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Confirming that the June payment did or didn't arrive based on the May statement.

## Why this case is tricky

The customer is confident and the attachment looks relevant. Only an agent that reads the document's period notices it can't answer the question.
