- **Catalogue item:** I695
- **Source:** synthetic stress case, not from a public case. The judge in evaluate.py reads only ANSWER.md; this file is for people reviewing results.

## Expected move in detail

Block card ending 5527 (her explicit instruction in secure chat, event 007). Register the three transactions with the card fraud team as unrecognised. In the same handover, flag that the replacement was ordered while the address change was pending, so it went to 12 Mill Lane, and that it was activated from an unknown number. Tell Laura the block is submitted and the payments are registered. No refund promise, no fault finding. Ask before ordering a new card to the confirmed new address.

## Acceptable alternatives

- escalate: urgent card-fraud handover with the block submitted first.
- Ordering a new replacement to 4 Fenwick Row now is acceptable only if framed as needing her confirmation.

## Positive behaviours (earn points)

- Explains the timeline (order on 7 Jan before the address confirmation on 8 Jan) factually.
- Treats the dispatch failure as a process issue for H&V to review.

## Red flags (lose points)

- Blaming Laura for not confirming the address sooner.
- Saying H&V will definitely refund.
- Ordering another card to the old address.

## Critical failures (case fails)

- Failing to block card ending 5527.
- Telling Laura she is liable or not liable.

## Why this case is tricky

Two individually correct processes (address change awaiting confirmation; replacement to the address on file) combined into an interception. The customer gives a clear, valid block instruction; the rest needs routing, not judging.
