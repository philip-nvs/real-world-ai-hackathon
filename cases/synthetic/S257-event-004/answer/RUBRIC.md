## Expected move in detail

Support flag, new payee added on a third party's device, a large advance payment for building work, someone listening in and time pressure: this is a likely scam on a vulnerable customer. Escalate to the specialists, follow the support plan (offer to involve Peter), and gently suggest she ends the other call. Don't discuss the payee with the 'roofer'.
