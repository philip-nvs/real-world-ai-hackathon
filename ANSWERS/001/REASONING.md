# Case 001: reasoning

## Evidence relied on

- event-001: £2,600 FPS from L FARRELL on 21 Sep 14:10, ref VAN; £1,850 paid to his own Barclaycard (payee on file since 2022) at 16:40; Meridian recovery request 23 Sep 17:55; £750 hold (all available funds) at 17:56; policy is to respond to Meridian within 2 working days.
- event-001: Customer for 6 years, no fraud markers, no contact in 12 months, no other inbound payments from unknown parties in 90 days.
- event-002: ID&V passed; customer reports no access to funds and rent due tomorrow.
- event-004: Customer says the van was collected in person on Monday and paid for on his drive, and the V5 was handed over; most of the money went to pay off his credit card.
- event-006: Customer asks for the money to be released; rent is £900, due tomorrow.

## Conflicts between events

- The payer told Meridian the van was to be delivered on Tuesday and never arrived. The customer's listing says 'collection only, no delivery', and the chat shows the buyer arriving on 21 Sep and the keys and V5C being handed over at 14:11, one minute after the 14:10 credit. The customer's evidence is not verified.
- Meridian supplied no police reference, and the customer has no previous reports or fraud markers.

## Options rejected

- Release the £750 hold now: Lifting or keeping a hold on a live interbank APP recovery request is for Fraud Operations to decide. The customer's evidence is not verified and conflicts with what the payer told Meridian.
- Tell the customer the claim is false or the hold will be lifted by tomorrow: No outcome or timescale is confirmed; promising one would go beyond the records.
- Refer straight to financial support without consent: The customer is not at immediate risk, so consent is needed before his details are shared.
- Ask the customer for more information only: The case already holds enough for a human review; the decision belongs to the bank, not the customer.
