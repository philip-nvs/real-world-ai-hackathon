# Case 010: ACT

## What the system received

A caller who says he is Dave Tilley of Tilley's Maintenance, and who banks with Meridian rather than Harrow & Vale, wants three things about our business customer Haldane Homes Ltd: which invoice their £90 payment on 1 Sep was for, whether they have the money to pay his overdue £1,400 invoice, and a direct number for whoever manages their account.

4 events; attachments: none.

## What the system did

No tool actions were taken.

## Decision

**ACT**: Tell the caller on the current business banking call that Harrow & Vale can't share any information about another customer's account. That covers payment details, the meaning of a reference, the balance or ability to pay, and internal contacts. Point him to Haldane Homes directly for which invoice the £90 covered and for his overdue invoice, and to his own bank, Meridian, for the details of the £90 he received.

## What was escalated

Nothing was escalated.

## What failed

Nothing failed.

## Final result

Nothing about Haldane Homes Ltd's account has been disclosed to the caller. He is being told on the call that the bank can't discuss another customer's account. He is pointed to Haldane Homes for which invoice the payment was for and for his overdue invoice, and to Meridian for the details of the payment he received.
