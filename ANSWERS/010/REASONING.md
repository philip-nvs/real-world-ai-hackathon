# Case 010: reasoning

## Evidence relied on

- event-004: The caller confirms he does not hold an account with Harrow & Vale; he banks with Meridian.
- event-001: Haldane Homes Ltd is an H&V business customer. The caller is identified as a third party, not an H&V customer. The details visible in Haldane's account (a £90 Faster Payment and other payments to TILLEYS MAINTENANCE) belong to Haldane and are confidential to them.
- event-002: The caller asks what the £90 reference means, whether Haldane can afford a £1,400 invoice, and for a direct number for Haldane's account contact.

## Conflicts between events

- The caller assumes that because our customer paid him, the bank can discuss that payment with him (event-004). The bank's duty of confidentiality is to Haldane Homes Ltd, its customer (event-001), and the caller is not authorised on that account.

## Options rejected

- Explain the reference or say which invoice the £90 was for, using Haldane's account view: This would disclose our customer's payment details to a third party who is not authorised on the account. The details of the payment he received are available from his own bank, Meridian, and the invoice it relates to from Haldane.
- Say whether Haldane has the money to pay the £1,400 invoice: This would disclose a customer's balance or creditworthiness to a third party. It is confidential and outside what the bank can share.
- Give a direct number for Haldane's relationship manager or account contact: Internal contact details for a customer's account are not shared with third parties. Any dispute over the invoice is between him and Haldane.
- Send a secure message: The caller is not a Harrow & Vale customer and has no secure portal. Telling Haldane would share nothing they need and isn't called for.
- Check Haldane's records or escalate to a team: No record check is needed to refuse disclosure, and looking further into the customer's account for a third party is not appropriate. Nothing suggests fraud or a complaint, so this is a routine confidentiality refusal.
