# Case 009: reasoning

## Evidence relied on

- event-001: Account view: balance £12.40; active DDs to Meridian Motor Insurance £68.20 and FitLife Gyms £45.00 (next 1 Oct); SO £200 to S PRICE on the 28th; employer expense credits still arriving (last £83.10, 14 Sep); no CASS request on file.
- event-001: Closure policy: an account with active DDs/SOs can be closed on instruction, but the bank must warn that they will fail; a CASS full switch is available at the new bank's request within 7 working days.
- event-002: Customer passed ID&V and asked to close the account today and receive the balance.
- event-004: Customer says nothing is left on the account and asks for later credits to be forwarded to his new account.

## Conflicts between events

- event-004: the customer says nothing is left on the account; event-001 and the account summary attachment show two active Direct Debits, an active standing order and recurring incoming payments.
- The insurer's letter (a customer-forwarded attachment, not verified) says the policy will be cancelled if a payment stays unpaid for 14 days; the Meridian DD is due 1 Oct.

## Options rejected

- Submit the closure instruction now, citing event-004: The customer gave the instruction believing nothing was attached. Policy requires the bank to warn him first that the active DDs and SO will fail, and the motor insurance could lapse after the 1 Oct collection.
- Set up forwarding of future credits to the new account: The bank doesn't do this manually. Redirection comes only through a CASS full switch requested by the new bank, and none is on file.
- Escalate to a closures or payment team: Nothing needs a team's judgement yet. The customer has to make an informed choice first.
