# Case 018: reasoning

## Evidence relied on

- event-001: The standing order is £500.00 on the 1st to HARROW & VALE RE LIME PROPERTY CA, ref "862A BEV RD". Balance £96, no overdraft. Salary stopped in July; irregular agency credits of £150–£300 since August.
- event-002: On a verified phone call (ID&V passed), the customer asked to change the amount to £400 from next month because of job loss, and mentioned a later increase to £550.
- event-003: The servicing agent told the customer they could change the standing order because it is his instruction.
- event-004: The customer says he knows £400 is less than the rent, has told the agency, and wants the change made.

## Conflicts between events

- event-003: the agent said the change could be made directly on the call. Under bank policy, a new standing-order amount must be confirmed by the customer in the secure portal after the bank sends it to him, so the phone request on its own is not enough.
- The standing-order journal check could not return a result (no live system connected), so the current status and next run date are not confirmed from the journal. They rest on the internal account view in event-001.

## Options rejected

- Submit the £400 amendment directly, citing the phone call in event-002/004: A new standing-order amount is a change the bank prepares for the customer to approve. It only counts once he confirms it in the secure portal after the bank sends it, so the phone request is the request, not the confirmation.
- Refer the customer to the financial support team right away: He is in financial hardship but not at immediate risk, so his consent is needed before his details are shared with a support service. The message asks for it.
- Set up the later increase to £550 now: It is a possible future request with no amount date or confirmation. The step is kept to the £400 change only.
